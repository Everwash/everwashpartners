EverWash is honored to acknowledge that Sean Pashley, our VP of Partner Success, has been elected to the Board of [Leadership Worth Following (LWF)](https://www.leadershipworthfollowing.org/), a nonprofit organization dedicated to developing authentic, values-driven leadership within the car wash industry.\
\
Pashley has spent his career at EverWash helping car wash operators build and scale successful membership programs, working directly with partners across the country to drive growth, retention, and long-term profitability.\
\
Known throughout the industry for his hands-on approach and commitment to operator success, Pashley also contributes articles to several car wash industry trade publications, as well as "At The Car Wash with Sean Pashley," a recurring segment on EverWash's podcast, ["Members Only,"](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLb_t4jhLuCPg1A7TUGDKeWfHdLWUSkmVj&si=csoVp8L_aOMZENvV) where he shares insights and best practices with car wash owners and operators nationwide.\
