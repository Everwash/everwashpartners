Episode 3 of *Members Only: The EverWash Podcast* is officially live — and this one is packed with insights for car wash operators preparing for a busy season and looking to grow their membership programs.

Hosted by Max Pulcini and Chris Villella, the podcast is a monthly conversation about membership, growth, and what actually works for operators running modern car washes.

This episode brings back several of our core segments, along with a great guest to help operators get ready for one of the biggest industry events of the year.

## **What You’ll Hear in Episode 3**

First up, we kick things off with **Wash Wire**, where we recap the SCWA Convention & Expo, including key takeaways from the show and conversations recorded right from the floor.

Next, we head to **At the Wash with Sean Pashley**, where Sean shares how operators can prepare for the busy pollen season ahead — and how to turn increased demand into long-term membership growth.

After that, we dive into **Data Drip**, where EverWash analyst Jane Lee breaks down what actually happens when operators change their membership pricing — and how those decisions impact conversions and revenue.

Finally, we sit down with Kendra Johnson, Chief Experience Officer at the [International Carwash Association](chatgpt://generic-entity?number=0), to preview **The Car Wash Show in Nashville** and share how operators can make the most of their time at the event.
