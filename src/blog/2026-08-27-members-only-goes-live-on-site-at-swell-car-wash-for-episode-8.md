Episode 8 of Members Only takes the show on location — live from Swell Car Wash in Galloway, NJ! \
Max Pulcini and Chris Villella are joined on-site by EverWash's Sean Pashley for a jam-packed episode: Sean puts Max in the driver's seat for a live membership sales role play, Chris sits down with Swell owner Lauren Hough to talk about her journey from the gas and petroleum business into car wash ownership (and where she's expanding next), and Sean catches up with site manager Bryce Malone on what it really takes to run a location day-to-day.
