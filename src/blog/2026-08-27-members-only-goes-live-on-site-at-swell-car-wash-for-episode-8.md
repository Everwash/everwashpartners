For Episode 8 of Members Only, we took the show on the road — recording live from Swell Car Wash in Galloway, New Jersey. It's a format shift for us, and the on-site energy paid off, especially in what owner Lauren Hough had to say about building a car wash business from an unlikely starting point.

We also sat down with Swell site manager Bryce Malone, who brought the conversation down to ground level — attendant motivation, day-to-day membership operations, and the promotions and contests Swell has tried to keep members engaged. And EverWash's Sean Pashley put co-host Max Pulcini through a live membership sales role play in a new segment we're calling At The Car Wash, plus caught member reactions straight from the line.
