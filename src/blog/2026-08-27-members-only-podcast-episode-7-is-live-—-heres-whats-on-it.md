**The data on referrals — and why it matters** Jane Lee is back for Data Drip, breaking down what referred members actually look like across tunnel, IBA, and self-serve locations — how long they stay, how often they wash, and what they're worth. If you're not actively using EverWash's referral program, this segment will change that.

**An operator story worth hearing** We sat down with Jon Dore of Summit Auto Spa, a multi-location operator in Northeast Pennsylvania who came into car washing through the tire business. His first wash struggled. EverWash helped turn it around. That success led to a second location — and now he's rebuilding the original site from the ground up. Jon also talks about managing membership alongside a tire operation and whether those two customer bases overlap more than you'd think.

**Wash Wire: We're heading to Fort Worth** EverWash will be at Booth 919 at the Southwest Car Wash Association Convention and Expo in Fort Worth, Texas — February 24–25, 2027. We also preview the fall trade show calendar, including Northeast Car Wash Con and the Kleen Rite Open House and Expo.

**Mailbag: When do you hire a dedicated membership salesperson?** Mike, a tunnel operator from Connecticut, wrote in after last month's episode with a question a lot of operators are sitting on. Chris and Max give him a straight answer.
