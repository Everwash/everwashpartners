Episode 4 of *Members Only: The EverWash Podcast* is live — and this one goes deep on something every membership program depends on but not enough operators think about: the people behind the program.

Hosted by Max Pulcini and Chris Villella, *Members Only* is a monthly conversation about car wash membership, growth, and what actually works for operators building sustainable, recurring-revenue businesses.

### What You'll Hear in Episode 4

We kick things off with **Wash Wire**, covering the latest news and updates from EverWash and Coinless.

Then we head to **At The Car Wash with Sean Pashley**, where Sean breaks down his "guerilla hiring" philosophy for building a summer sales team that can actually sell memberships. The core message: stop waiting until you're short-staffed to start hiring. Treat recruiting like a daily discipline, prioritize personality over experience, and build depth in your roster — because one great attendant calling out on a busy Saturday in June is real money walking out the door.

After that, we welcome **Sue Lorenzon and Kate Lorenzon** to the studio. Sue and Kate are EverWash's Membership Support Managers — sisters-in-law, friends for over two decades, and two of the most important people in the EverWash operation. They talk about what great member support actually looks like, the most common reasons members cancel and how to prevent it, how professional support directly impacts lifetime value and churn, and where AI fits in — and where it doesn't — when the goal is keeping real people subscribed.

Finally, we call up **Steve Chill of Chill's EZ Kleen** in New Castle, Pennsylvania. Steve runs a triple IBA with self-serve bays, a dog wash, a reclaimed tunnel, and a third location he opened with business partner Luke Smith. His membership revenue has grown every single month since joining EverWash in July 2023. He's positioned his operation so effectively that a competitor who had already broken ground in New Castle turned around and walked away. And every October, he transforms his car wash into one of the most talked-about haunted attractions in western Pennsylvania — lines stretching for hours, props built from scraps and trash, local news cameras rolling. Every. Single. Year.

It's one of the best operator stories we've told on this show.
