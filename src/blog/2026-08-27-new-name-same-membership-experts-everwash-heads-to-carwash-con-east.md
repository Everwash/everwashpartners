The Northeast Regional Carwash Convention has a new name. Starting this year, the East Coast's biggest carwash show is Carwash Con East — same Atlantic City Convention Center, same operators, same three days that a lot of us plan our fall around.

What hasn't changed on our end: EverWash will be there, and we'll be talking membership.

You'll find us at Booth #324 when the show floor opens Tuesday, October 6.

### What We're Bringing to Booth #324

Three things we've been building are ready to put in front of you:

* Fleet: Every market has businesses running vehicles through somebody's wash. Fleet gives you a way to land those accounts — local contractors, delivery outfits, municipal vehicles, community organizations — and fold them into the membership program you already run.
* Quick Scan QR Codes: The gap between a customer who is interested and a customer who is enrolled is where most memberships die. Quick Scan closes it, turning a car in your lot into a member without the friction that usually gets in the way.
* Updated Attendant App: Your attendants are your sales team, whether or not anyone calls them that. The new app is built for them: a purpose-built tool for selling and managing memberships from the ground, not from an office.

### A Look at What's Next

We'll also be walking partners and operators through the features coming to the platform after this show. If you have opinions about what we should build next — and most operators do — the booth is the place to share them. A lot of what ships at EverWash starts as something an operator told us at a trade show.

### Real Numbers from Real Operators

The most useful thing we can show you probably isn't a product. It's what other washes are actually doing with the program: how they grew recurring revenue, what their retention looks like, and what they'd do differently starting over. We'll have those stories at the booth. Ask for the ones that match your setup.

### When and Where to Find Us

Carwash Con East runs October 5-7, 2026 at the Atlantic City Convention Center. EverWash will be on the show floor both days it's open:

* **Tuesday, October 6: 1:00 PM - 6:00 PM**
* **Wednesday, October 7: 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM**
