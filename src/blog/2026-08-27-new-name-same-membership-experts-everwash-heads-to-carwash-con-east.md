### We Know Fleet. So We Built It Differently.

Fleet business isn’t new to the car wash industry. But that doesn’t mean the way operators manage it can’t be better.

**EverWash Fleet was developed with more than 50 years of combined fleet-industry experience behind it**. We didn’t start by asking what features we could add. We started with how fleet relationships actually work, where the friction exists, and how technology could make the entire process easier for both the operator and their customer.

The result is a platform that puts the operator in control. Build and manage your own fleet accounts, add and manage vehicles, monitor usage, and work directly with your commercial customers, all through one simple platform.

**Your fleet customers. Your relationships. Your program. Powered by EverWash.**

For contractors, service companies, delivery fleets, municipalities and other organizations already washing vehicles in your market, EverWash Fleet gives you the technology to make doing business with your wash easier.

Because the opportunity isn’t new.

What’s new is how simple we’re making it to capture, manage and grow it.

### Sell Memberships Even When Nobody Is There to Sell Them

Unattended washes have always faced an obvious problem:

**How do you sell a membership when there’s no one there to make the sale?**

EverWash Quick Scan was built to solve it.

A customer arrives at the wash, scans a QR code, joins from their phone and can use their membership immediately.

**No attendant. No app download required to join. No waiting.**

For in-bay automatic and other unattended locations, Quick Scan turns the physical wash site itself into a membership sales channel.

Put the opportunity in front of the customer when it matters most: **when they’re already there to wash their car.**

### Give Your Attendants a Better Way to Sell

At staffed locations, technology should make attendants better at selling, not give them another system to fight with.

The **updated EverWash Attendant App** gives frontline employees a purpose-built tool for selling and managing memberships where the customer interaction actually happens at the wash.

Because your attendants aren’t just processing cars.

**They’re standing in front of your next member.**
