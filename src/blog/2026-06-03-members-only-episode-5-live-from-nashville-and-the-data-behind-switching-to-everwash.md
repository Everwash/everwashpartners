The ICA Car Wash Show comes around once a year, and for EverWash, it's always one of the most energizing weeks on the calendar. This year in Nashville, we decided to bring the podcast with us.

Episode 5 of **Members Only: The EverWash Podcast** was recorded live from the EverWash booth on the show floor — and between the conversations, the energy, and live performances, it turned into one of our best episodes yet. Check it out here:

**In this episode:**

* EverWash CEO & Founder Scott Caplan on where the industry is headed and what operators are talking about on the show floor
* VP of Partner Success Sean Pashley on what separates operators who succeed with membership from those who struggle
* Voices from the Floor — quick hits with Ofer Sadka ([Ask Emma](https://www.askemma.io/)) and Ali & Conor ([UpLead Digital](https://upleaddigital.com/))
* Data Drip with Jane Lee — the data behind switching to EverWash from an existing or underperforming membership program, including three real case studies
