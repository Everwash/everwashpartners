One of the simplest ways car wash operators can grow their membership revenue isn’t necessarily finding new customers — it’s enrolling additional vehicles from the customers they already have.

Family plans make this possible.

By allowing members to add multiple vehicles to a single account at a discounted rate, operators create an easy path for customers to expand their membership. The result is higher lifetime value, stronger retention, and increased recurring revenue.

And the data backs it up.

## Family Plans Create Revenue Without Additional Acquisition Costs

One of the biggest advantages of family plan discounts is that they generate additional revenue without additional marketing or acquisition costs.

Members who already trust your wash are simply adding another vehicle to their account.

As EverWash data analyst Jane Lee recently explained on the [Members Only: The EverWash Podcast](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLb_t4jhLuCPg1A7TUGDKeWfHdLWUSkmVj&si=0z6OJEfMf693joeL):

“One of the biggest benefits of the family plan discount is that it generates another income stream with no additional administrative efforts or acquisition cost.”

Even better, the process is extremely easy for customers. With EverWash, additional vehicles can be added either:

* Directly inside the EverWash mobile app
* On-site with an attendant using the attendant sales app

This convenience removes friction and encourages members to expand their accounts.

## Family Plan Members Stay Longer

A common concern operators have about family plan discounts is whether offering a discounted additional vehicle impacts overall profitability.

According to EverWash platform data, the opposite is true.

At tunnel car washes, family plan members perform just as well — and in some cases slightly better — than additional vehicles at locations that do not offer the family plan discount.

Family plan members stay on about 11 months, generating roughly $282 in revenue, compared to about 9 months and $262 at locations without the discount.

At in-bay automatic (IBA) locations, the difference is even more noticeable.

Family plan members stay on about 10 months, generating $210, compared to 7 months and $159 at locations that do not offer the discount.

In other words, offering a small recurring discount often improves overall retention and revenue.

## Multi-Vehicle Accounts Are Even More Valuable

The real impact of family plans becomes clear when looking at multi-membership accounts as a whole.

Customers with multiple vehicles enrolled tend to be significantly more loyal.

At tunnel locations, the primary membership on a multi-vehicle account stays active for about 15.5 months, generating roughly $490 in revenue, compared to 9 months and $301 for single memberships.

That’s nearly $200 more revenue per member.

At IBA locations, the primary membership stays active for about 13 months, generating $337, compared to 9 months and $243 for single memberships.

As Jane Lee explains:

“Multi-membership accounts create more loyal customers because they see the value in keeping all their vehicles enrolled.”

Once multiple cars are tied to the same account, the membership becomes part of the household routine.

## Make Family Plans Easy for Customers

For family plans to succeed, operators need to make them simple for customers to understand and easy to enroll in.

EverWash partners can enable family plans directly within the platform, allowing customers to manage multiple vehicles through the EverWash app or with assistance from attendants.

Recent updates to the EverWash attendant app also allow on-site staff to easily add vehicles to an existing account, helping convert everyday wash visits into expanded memberships.

Combined with built-in analytics in the EverWash Owner Portal, operators can track the performance of family plan discounts and monitor how multi-vehicle memberships impact retention and revenue.

## A Simple Way to Grow Membership Revenue

Growing a membership program doesn’t always require more advertising or more traffic. Sometimes the biggest opportunity is already in your customer base.

By making it easy for members to add additional vehicles, family plans increase revenue per household while strengthening long-term retention.
